# Community audit of the 21 builds, 2026-10-13/14

## Why
The builds were derived from the wiki: what each item does, read against each kit. The wiki is the right source for
mechanics and numbers, but it does not say what experienced players actually pick. A pilot on Bandit, Huntress and
Commando showed the same problem in all three, and the full audit found it in almost every build.

## Method
- One researcher per survivor gathered player sources: YouTube guides, tier lists and Eclipse 8 runs (read through
  their transcripts), Steam Community guides and threads, and official patch notes. Reddit was not reachable by the
  research tools.
- Each source got a date, the game era it covers and a quality rating. SEO listicles and AI-written pages (Rogue
  Ranker, GameRant-style guides) are listed in the reports as low and never decided anything.
- A priority changed only when 3+ credible sources agreed. Two sources allow Média/Situacional with an honest `why`.
  One source goes to Situacional or `notes`. When players split, the build recommends the majority and keeps the
  other choice as a `Variante` with when to pick it.
- Mechanics and numbers still come from the wiki. Where players and the wiki disagree on a mechanic, the build keeps
  the wiki and writes the disagreement in `why` or `notes`.
- The research reports (sources tables, item-by-item divergences) were kept outside the repository; each build's
  `sources` lists the ones its choices rest on, tagged by `kind` and `date`.

## What the audit found (recurring)
1. **One wrong skill per build, often two.** Huntress primary, Commando secondary, Bandit special, Loader secondary,
   Engineer secondary and utility, Mercenary utility, Artificer secondary and special, Captain beacon, Acrid
   secondary, Railgunner special, False Son special, Seeker secondary, CHEF secondary and special (the build was
   still the pre-rework kit), Drifter special, Operator special, MUL-T loadout.
2. **"On paper" essentials.** Lens-Maker's Glasses, Ocular HUD, Laser Scope, Primordial Cube, Gorag's Opus,
   Sentient Meat Hook and Leeching Seed were core in many builds without player backing. Lens-Maker's on Bandit
   (Backstab already crits) and Railgunner (about +5% per stack) were the clearest cases.
3. **Missing player staples.** Kjaro's and Runald's Bands (as a pair), Crowbar, mobility (Hopoo Feather, Goat Hoof,
   Energy Drink, Mocha), Repulsion Armor Plate, Gasoline / Will-o'-the-wisp.
4. **Void contradictions.** Builds rated both an item and the Void item that corrupts it (Polylute and Ukulele,
   Plasma Shrimp and AtG, Needletick and Tri-Tip, Singularity Band and the Bands). Every build now states the trade.
5. **Exceptions are real.** Backup Magazine is a staple on Loader, Artificer, Acrid, Operator, Enforcer and Heretic
   (extra charges of a key skill) but a trap on Railgunner (extra shots skip the perfect-reload bonus).

## Review again
- **Enforcer and Acolyte:** in 3 to 6 weeks, when creator guides and the first balance patch are out.
- **Operator and Drifter:** when more post-December-2025 material exists.
- **Hallowed Concepts skills** (Laser Cyclone, Sabotage, Cloying Breath, Tectonic Blast, Laser Cutter,
  Proliferation): listed in `notes` only, until players evaluate them.
